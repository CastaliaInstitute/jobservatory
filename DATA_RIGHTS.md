# Data rights and reuse notice

The software source code in this repository is licensed under the MIT License. That license does **not** grant rights in third-party job-listing content, employer names, source metadata, or evidence excerpts.

Jobservatory retrieves public career feeds and publishes a limited research representation: source metadata, cryptographic hashes, derived fields, and short evidence excerpts linked to the source. Full descriptions are not republished. Every configured source currently has `rightsReviewStatus: pending`; access to a public endpoint is not treated as permission to redistribute content or train models.

Before adding a source to a public release, record the governing terms URL and version/date, permitted retrieval and retention, excerpt policy, redistribution status, model-training status, attribution requirements, reviewer, and review date. Until that review is complete, downstream users should treat the corpus as a research demonstration and should follow source links rather than redistributing excerpts.

O*NET-derived fields are subject to the [O*NET database license](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html) and include taxonomy attribution in the exported record.
